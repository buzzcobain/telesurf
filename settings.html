⚙ TELESURF SETTINGS

Regional Teletext Themes

Ceefax (UK) The World System Teletext standard. Cyan/Yellow on Black.
Antiope / Minitel (France) Amber and Blue inspired by the French packet-switching system.
ARD Videotext (Germany) High contrast Magenta and Red headers.
NOS Teletekst (Netherlands) Famous blue backgrounds with blocky yellow headers.
Televideo (Italy) RAI style red and blue distinct graphic blocks.
SVT Text (Sweden) Clean black background with heavy cyan and yellow typography.
Teletexto (Spain) TVE style green and yellow typography.
